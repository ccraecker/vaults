**Affordability**: HDB flats are designed to be affordable for most Singaporeans.

**Government Support**: First-time buyers can benefit from various grants and subsidies, making it easier to own a home early in your careers. [[Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (Families)].(opens in a new tab)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/buying-a-flat/understanding-your-eligibility-and-housing-loan-options/flat-and-grant-eligibility/couples-and-families/enhanced-cpf-housing-grant-families)

**Community Living**: HDB estates often foster a strong sense of community, with amenities like markets, parks, and community centers.


# **HDB buying journey**

Before starting the HDB buying process, it is important that you recognise that the total cost goes far beyond the flat’s listed price.

Many first-time buyers underestimate additional expenses such as Buyer’s Stamp Duty, legal fees, renovation, and moving costs which could amount to tens of thousands of dollars.

Refer to this HDB Flat Buying Journey infographic below for an overview of the key steps and considerations in the process. You can learn more about the buying process [here.](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/buying-a-flat)

![[Pasted image 20260815200222.png]]

1. **Check your eligibility and finance first** - Apply for an [HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter(opens in a new tab)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/cs/infoweb/residential/buying-a-flat/understanding-your-eligibility-and-housing-loan-options/application-for-an-hdb-flat-eligibility-hfe-letter) to determine your household’s eligibility—including flat types, CPF grants, and loan limits—before starting your search.
    
2. 2
    
    **Take advantage of housing grants** - (1) [Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG)(opens in a new tab)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/cs/infoweb/residential/buying-a-flat/understanding-your-eligibility-and-housing-loan-options/flat-and-grant-eligibility/couples-and-families/enhanced-cpf-housing-grant-families): Up to S$120,000 for first-time families buying (up to S$60,000 if one partner is a second-timer). (2) [CPF Housing Grants for Resale Flats(opens in a new tab)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/cs/infoweb/residential/buying-a-flat/understanding-your-eligibility-and-housing-loan-options/flat-and-grant-eligibility/couples-and-families/cpf-housing-grants-for-resale-flats-families): Up to S$80,000, plus additional Proximity Grants, subject to income caps. (3) [Proximity Housing Grant (Families)(opens in a new tab)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/cs/infoweb/residential/buying-a-flat/understanding-your-eligibility-and-housing-loan-options/flat-and-grant-eligibility/couples-and-families/proximity-housing-grant-families) - Up to $30,000 to live with your parents or up to $20,000 to live near your parents (within 4km).
    
3. 3
    
    **Understand all purchase costs** - Budget for stamp duty, legal fees, option fees, renovation, and down-payment—plus the initial and ongoing monthly mortgage installments.
    
4. 4
    
    **Leverage Priority Schemes** - Schemes like the [Family & Parenthood Priority Scheme, Married Child Priority Scheme, and Multi-Generation Priority Scheme(opens in a new tab)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/buying-a-flat/buying-procedure-for-new-flats/application/priority-schemes) increase your chance to book popular flats, especially for families.
    
5. 5
    
    **Plan your housing budget Carefully** - Consider all sources: cash, CPF savings, and eligible grants. You can also use HDB’s [budget calculator(opens in a new tab)](https://homes.hdb.gov.sg/home/calculator/budget) to help you with the math. The calculator pulls information from your approved HFE letter (if any) by logging in, so you don’t have to enter the information again!
