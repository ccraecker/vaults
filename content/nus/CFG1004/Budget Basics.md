##   50-30-20 budgeting rule

The **50-30-20** rule, developed by U.S. Senator and bankruptcy expert Elizabeth Warren, is a simple way to manage your budget.

It suggests dividing your take-home pay (after CPF deductions in Singapore) into:

- 50% for needs,
    
- 30% for wants, and
    
- 20% for savings.
    

This helps you make the most of your **disposable income**. 

The 50-30-20 budget is simple to use, but it may not make sense for everyone, especially if your essential expenses exceed 50% of your take-home pay or you are managing significant debt like student loans.

If that is the case, **adjust the percentages to fit your situation**. You might choose to cut back on wants for a period or find ways to increase your income.

Remember, these changes can be temporary. You decide how long they last, and you can adjust your budget as your debt decreases or income grows.
