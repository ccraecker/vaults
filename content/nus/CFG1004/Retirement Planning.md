## What is Supplementary Retirement Scheme?

The Supplementary Retirement Scheme (SRS) is part of the Government's multi-pronged strategy to address the financial needs of a greying population. It is a **voluntary scheme** that complements the CPF.

Participants can contribute a varying amount to SRS (subject to a cap) at their own discretion. The contributions may be used to purchase various investment instruments. The SRS offers attractive tax benefits. Contributions to SRS are eligible for tax relief, investment returns are accumulated tax-free (with the exception of Singapore dividends), and only 50% of the withdrawals from SRS are taxable at retirement.


## Why start now?

More Time = More Growth
	When you start young, your savings have more time to compound. CPF Special Account (SA) balances earn up to 4–5% interest per year, risk-free, meaning the money you set aside today can grow significantly over decades. **Note:** this is just one option, there are many tools for retirement planning as mentioned earlier

Tax Savings Now, Security Later
	Top-ups to CPF and contributions to SRS reduce your taxable income, giving you immediate tax relief—up to $16,000 per year from CPF top-ups and $15,300 from SRS (for SCs and PRs). You save money today and build up your future retirement funds.

Lower Stress, More Flexibility in the Future
	By starting early, you could reduce the pressure to “catch up” later. You will have more financial flexibility, could be choosing when to retire, pursuing a passion project, or handling unexpected expenses like medical costs.


**Note:** CPF (Central Provident Fund) is a key part of Singapore’s social support system, helping locals with retirement, housing, and healthcare.

If you're an international student, CPF may not apply to you, but the principle of starting early to take advantage of compounding and making the most of available options is relevant wherever you work.
