**Step 1: Update your contact details and notice preferences**

Head to [myTax Portal(opens in a new tab)](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/login/selection) and make sure your contact details and notice preferences are up to date.

This helps you get timely notices, reminders, and important letters from IRAS.

**Step 2: Understand the tax season**

Here is how the timeline usually goes:

1. **Feb to Mar**: IRAS sends out filing notifications for the Year of Assessment* (YA).
    
2. **1 Mar to 18 Apr**: If you are required to file, this is your window to e-File your tax return.
    
3. **End Apr onwards**: Most people will start receiving their tax bills (also known as Notice of Assessment).

***What is a Year of Assessment (YA)?**

The YA is the year in which income tax is charged on the income earned in the preceding calendar year, which is known as the basis year.

So, if it is YA 2026, IRAS is taxing the income you earned from 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2025.

**Step 3: Determine Your Tax Filing Requirements**

Not everyone has to file a tax return. It depends on your situation. You’ll fall into one of these three groups below.


## You need to file

You will receive a notice asking you to submit your Income Tax Return.

## No-Filing Service (NFS)

If you qualify for NFS, you do not need to file unless you are making adjustments to your income details or relief claims. IRAS will pre-fill your income details received from your employer in your tax return.

Please log in to myTax Portal to verify the pre-filled income information You will need to inform IRAS through filing if you wish to make changes.
 
## Direct Notice of Assessment (D-NOA)

You get your tax bill directly based on your income provided by your employer and previous year’s relief claim, if any.

No filing is needed, but it is still important to check your NOA and update IRAS if there is any inaccurate information.

**Step 4: Review your tax bill**

After your return is processed (or if you are on NFS or D-NOA), you will receive your Tax Bill. Here's what to do:

1. Review Your Tax Bill.
    
2. Pay your outstanding income taxes in full or apply for monthly instalment through GIRO*.
    
3. If you wish to make changes to your income and reliefs, you may do so via the ‘Amend your tax bill’ digital service on myTax Portal within 30 days from the date stated in your income tax bill.
    

_*_**_Pro-tip!_** _Opting for GIRO payment allows you to enjoy up to 12 interest-free monthly instalments, enabling you to keep more money in your account earning interest rather than paying everything upfront._

**Tax Clearance Process**

For **work pass holders** (like an Employment Pass or Personalised Employment Pass holder), there is something called **tax clearance** in Singapore.

When you leave your job or plan to leave Singapore for good, your employer must inform IRAS and settle your taxes before you go. This is known as filing for tax clearance.

And yes, it's the **employer’s** responsibility to do it.

_*Note that this does not apply to Singapore citizens._
