**What is Taxation?**

Let us start with the basics. [**Taxation**(opens in a new tab)](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/t/taxation.asp) is a term for when a taxing authority, usually a government, levies or imposes a financial obligation on its citizens or residents.  

Why do taxes exist? Think of taxes as the foundation that keeps Singapore thriving.

At the national level, taxes support four key areas:

1. **Social development**: Funding education, healthcare, and community support programmes
    
2. **Security and external relations**: Supporting defence, home affairs, and diplomacy
    
3. **Economic development**: Creating conditions for businesses to grow and innovate
    
4. **Government administration**: Ensuring efficient delivery of public services unity
