## Option Fee

An Option to Purchase (OTP) is a **legal contract** between the buyer and seller of a property.

By paying the option fee, the buyer **reserves** the property and gains **exclusive rights** to purchase it within a set period, during which the seller cannot sell to others. If the buyer backs out of the deal, the option fee is forfeited. During this period, the buyer can inspect the property and arrange financing.

To proceed, the buyer pays an option exercise fee, which goes toward the down payment.

The amount required varies depending on whether you’re buying an HDB BTO, resale flat, or a private property.

1. For an overview of the option fees for different types of **HDB BTO flats** [https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/buying-a-flat/buying-procedure-for-new-flats/booking-of-flat(opens in a new tab)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/buying-a-flat/buying-procedure-for-new-flats/booking-of-flat)
    
2. For **HDB resale flats**, these are the typical option fees. The buyer has 21 days to exercise the OTP. [https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/selling-a-flat/plan-source-and-contract/option-to-purchase(opens in a new tab)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/selling-a-flat/plan-source-and-contract/option-to-purchase)
    
3. As for private properties, the option fee is typically 1%, with an additional 4-9% required to exercise it. The buyer has 14 days to exercise the OTP. These terms are still negotiable.


## Downpayment

The downpayment is the **difference between the purchase price of the property and the loan amount you are granted, minus any option fees you have already paid**.

The percentage of the down payment required depends on whether you are taking an HDB loan or a bank loan.




## Buyer Stamp Duty (BSD) and Additional Buyer’s Stamp Duty (ABSD)

These are two main types of stamp duties: [Buyer’s Stamp Duty (BSD) and Additional Buyer’s Stamp Duty (ABSD)(opens in a new tab)](https://www.iras.gov.sg/quick-links/tax-rates/stamp-duty).

- **BSD** is a mandatory tax that all buyers must pay, regardless of whether they are Singapore citizens, permanent residents (PRs), or foreigners. The amount is calculated based on the purchase price or market value of the property, whichever is higher.
    
- **ABSD** is an **additional tax** that may apply depending on the buyer’s residency status and the number of properties they own.


## Agent Commissions

Property agent fees in Singapore vary depending on the type of property and services provided.

- For **HDB resale flats**, buyers typically pay a **1% commission** to the agent, which covers marketing, viewings, and paperwork.
    
- In **private property transactions**, **buyers usually do not pay commission**, as fees are shared between the seller’s and buyer’s agents. There are no fixed rates for agent fees—these are negotiable and may increase if more work is involved, such as urgent sales or handling overseas buyers.


## Property Valuation Fee

Both HDB and financial institutions assess the property's value before granting a loan to ensure the loan amount matches its true worth

- For **HDB resale flats**, you can submit a request for value with a fee of $120.
    
- For **private properties**, the cost of a valuation can range between $200 to $500. However, this fee might be included as part of a loan package offered by some financial institutions
