# HDB
**Affordability**: HDB flats are designed to be affordable for most Singaporeans.

**Government Support**: First-time buyers can benefit from various grants and subsidies, making it easier to own a home early in your careers. [[Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (Families)].(opens in a new tab)](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/buying-a-flat/understanding-your-eligibility-and-housing-loan-options/flat-and-grant-eligibility/couples-and-families/enhanced-cpf-housing-grant-families)

**Community Living**: HDB estates often foster a strong sense of community, with amenities like markets, parks, and community centers.


# Executive Condominium
**Eligibility**: Singaporeans or PRs meeting certain income caps.

**Price**: More expensive than HDBs but cheaper than private condos and come with a 5-year Minimum Occupation Period which doesn’t apply to private properties.

**Investment potential**: Good value over the long term.

# Private Housing
**Amenities**: Private properties often come with facilities like swimming pools, gyms, and enhanced security.

**Privacy and Prestige**: They offer more privacy and are often seen as a status symbol.

**Cost:** These properties are significantly more expensive, both in terms of purchase price and maintenance fees.
