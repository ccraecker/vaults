

## High upfront and ongoing costs

As of 2024, the total cost of owning a modest brand new car in Singapore can exceed S$150,000, largely due to the Certificate of Entitlement (COE), which alone can cost over S$100,000. This does not include ongoing costs such as insurance, road tax, petrol, maintenance, and parking.

## Income levels and affordability

Financial benchmarks suggest that individuals earning below S$5,000–S$6,000 per month may find it challenging to manage car ownership sustainably without impacting other financial priorities. While some guidelines recommend keeping car-related expenses within 20% of monthly take-home pay, this can overlap with major commitments like housing. It may also affect your Total Debt Servicing Ratio (TDSR), as discussed in the earlier housing section, which limits how much of your income can go toward all loan repayments.


## Ownership vs. usage

Singapore’s efficient public transport, car-sharing platforms, and second-hand car market offer alternatives that may meet mobility needs without the full financial burden of ownership.
