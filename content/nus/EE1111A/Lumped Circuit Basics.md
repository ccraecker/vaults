Lumped circuit model: A simplified representation of physical circuit using **discrete** circuit elements. 
	
	**Discrete means that any component only has one thing (eg. resistance).** 
	
	Each element is represented by its terminal voltage-current relationship.
	
	Ideal wires have no resistance and negligible propagation delay.
	
	Resistors in SG are squiggly lines.

Reality: Physical circuits are not ideal.
	
	Components have tolerance and parasitic effects.
		tolerance is like the range the actual value of something will be found in
		 (a 100 ohm resistor might actually have 105.3452 ohms of resistance)

		parasitic effects is like stuff acting as other stuff a bit idk
	Wires have finite resistance, inductance, and capacitance.
	Electrical signals propagate at a finite delay.
