Ohm's law: $V = IR$
V: Voltage (Volts)
I: Current (Amps)
R: Resistance (Ohms)

Current is water going through a pipe.
Voltage is what causes the water to flow (pressure on either side of the pipe)
Resistance is the property of the medium that causes the water flow to be slower
