Basically a thingy with a bunch of holes that can accept jumper wires
Internally, a breadboard has electrical connections between some sets of its sockets.



![[Pasted image 20260816204411.png]]

On the two sides are rails, which are two columns of pins that are each electrically connected all the way down. Left and right are not connected to each other. Every socket running down the same column is connected.

**By convention, we put a ground signal zero voltage to be the black/blue line (left)**
**And we put something like 5V or 9V on the red column**
![[Pasted image 20260816204612.png|244]]
The middle is connected in rows of 5. Things across the trough are not connected to each other. There's also no electrical connection between consecutive sets of rows.
